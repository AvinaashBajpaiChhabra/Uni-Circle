Senior Consultant

Dr. Prashant Bajpai

Dr. Prashant Bajpai is a highly experienced orthopedic surgeon based in Pitampura, Delhi, with over four decades of dedicated service in musculoskeletal care. He earned his MBBS from Maulana Azad Medical College and MS in Orthopaedics from Vardhman Mahavir Medical College & Safdarjung Hospital, and is a valued member of leading orthopedic professional associations. At Dr. Bajpai’s Spine & Ortho Pain Centre, he specializes in comprehensive treatments including fracture management, joint and bone care, pain relief, and rehabilitative physiotherapy. Known for his compassionate approach and clinical excellence, Dr. Bajpai is committed to personalized patient care and optimal recovery outcomes.
