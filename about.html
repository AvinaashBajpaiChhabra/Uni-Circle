Dr. Prashant Bajpai

Senior Consultant Orthopedic Surgeon

At Spine & Ortho Pain Centre, we offer services that combine the latest medical advancements with clinically tested results. Our goal is to provide you with the best quality of life possible in the most personable way. The Centre is run by a dedicated team comprising a Senior Consultant Orthopedic Surgeon, an expert postgraduate physiotherapist, and well-trained technical staff.

Dr. Prashant Bajpai, a leading Senior Consultant Orthopedic Surgeon with vast experience of working in the field of orthopedics for about 30 years, is the inspiration behind the edifice. He is a graduatefrom Maulana Azad Medical College, New Delhi (Batch 1976) and a Post Graduate in Orthopedics (M.S Orthopedics) (Batch 1983) from Safdarjang Hospital, New Delhi.

He worked in different capacities in various big government and private hospitals in Delhi, including LNJP and associated GB Pant Hospital, Safdarjang Hospital, AIIMS, and Ram Manohar Lohia Hospital. His extensive career also includes service at Sarvodya Hospital, Jaipur Golden Hospital, MGS Hospital, Bhagwan Mahavir Hospital, and Santom Hospital as a Senior Orthopedic Surgeon.

LNJP GB PANT SAFDARJANG HOSPITAL AIIMS RML Hospital SARVODYA HOSPITAL JAIPUR GOLDEN HOSPITAL MGS HOSPITAL BHAGWAN MAHAVIR HOSPITAL SANTOM HOSPITAL

He acquired training in Advance Pain Management procedures at Kolkata and Mumbai under the guidance of eminent pain specialists from India, Australia, UK, and the USA. He is also trained in Medical Ozone Therapy from the Ozone Forum of India, Mumbai. He is the only Orthopedician in North-West Delhi performing many advance Interventional Pain Management procedures and Medical Oxygen Ozone treatments for joints, spine, and various neurological or old-age related diseases.

Mission Statement

He believes "Real Pain Relief Is A Basic Human Right", dedicating his mission to relieving pain in society with his level best effort.

Most patients are treated at our Centre; however, for procedures requiring spinal or general anesthesia, patients are admitted to nearby well-equipped modern hospitals.
